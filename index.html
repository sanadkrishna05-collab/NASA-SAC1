NASA & Partner Data Resources

Explore open Earth-observation and agricultural datasets that can strengthen field context and rotation analysis.

OPEN DATA CATALOGUE · 25 SOURCES
How to use this catalogue

Search by dataset, topic, provider or geographic coverage. Availability and spatial resolution vary. Several USDA and USGS resources listed here are specific to the United States and should not be treated as locally applicable soil or crop data for farms in India.

25 datasets
NASAGlobal

NASA SMAP

Soil moisture

Surface soil moisture and freeze/thaw measurements every 2–3 days. Supports drought-stress, water-availability, infiltration and early crop-stress context.

NASAGlobal

NASA GPM / IMERG

Rainfall

Precipitation rate and accumulation estimates for rainfall reliability, storm frequency, erosion risk and water inputs. IMERG imagery can be explored through NASA Worldview.

NASA / USGSGlobal

Landsat

Satellite imagery

30 m corrected reflectance and vegetation indicators for crop vigor, bare ground, seasonal transitions and crop-stress mapping.

NASA / NOAAGlobal

MODIS / VIIRS

Vegetation indices

250–1000 m reflectance, NDVI and vegetation indicators for seasonal change and broad crop-condition context.

NASAGlobal

NASA POWER

Climate

Solar and meteorological datasets, including temperature and radiation, for growing-season conditions, crop-water-use context and climate suitability.

NASA / USGSGlobal

SRTM

Terrain

Elevation and terrain models for slope, runoff, erosion risk, water flow and terrain-related stress.

NASASelected coverage

ECOSTRESS

Land temperature / ET

Land-surface temperature and evapotranspiration at high spatial resolution to identify heat hotspots, plant stress and crop-water demand.

USDAUnited States

USDA Ag Data Commons

Agricultural datasets

Open datasets covering soils, crops, climate, land management, phenology, water quality and ecological conditions.

USDA NRCSUnited States

Web Soil Survey (WSS)

Soils

Official soil maps and properties, drainage, erosion risk, hydrologic ratings and land-use suitability.

USDA NASSUnited States

Cropland Data Layer (CDL) / CropScape

Crop classification

Annual 30 m cropland classifications for crop types and seasonal cropping-pattern analysis.

Oregon State UniversityUnited States

PRISM Climate Data

Climate

Daily, monthly and 30-year climate normals for precipitation, temperature, dewpoint and frost/freeze dates.

USDA NRCSUnited States

SSURGO / STATSGO2

Soils

Field- and regional-scale soil attributes including texture, organic matter, drainage, hydrologic group, erosion factors and water-holding capacity.

USDAUnited States

Aerial imagery

Typically 1 m aerial imagery for field boundaries, bare-soil periods, crop development and land-management changes.

USGSUnited States

National Land Cover Database (NLCD)

Land cover

30 m multi-decade land-cover classifications for farmland, pasture, forest, water, wetlands and development.
